## Theme
**Literacia e Segurança do Doente**, with **ULSBE (Barcelos/Esposende)**.
Real problem, real partner. Build a mobile app for ULS users that helps them
participate safely and informed in their care.

Why it matters (from PA01):
1. Misunderstood diagnoses → delayed treatment, anxiety
2. Missed alarm signs → late response to complications
3. Poor medication adherence → wrong doses, abandonment
4. Inequality → low digital/health literacy = more exposed to errors
5. Patient involvement = OMS pillar for clinical safety

## PA02 — Iniciação: Proposta de sistema (received 2026-09-22)
Original: `moodle-originals/projeto-aplicado/PA02 - Iniciação Projeto.pdf` (11 slides).
This is the **first structured deliverable**: a system proposal that must answer six blocks, in PT:

1. **Identificação do problema** — which concrete health-literacy / patient-safety problem; who is most affected
   among ULSBE users (idosos, low schooling, chronic patients, others); at which point of the patient journey
   (before consultation, during admission, at discharge, managing medication at home).
2. **Utilizadores e contexto de uso** — patient, family/carer, or both; what digital/health-literacy limits the
   target audience has and how that shapes the interface; where and when the app is used (home, waiting room, hospital).
3. **Proposta de funcionalidades** — which **2–3 main features** solve the problem; how the app communicates health
   information simply (language, images, video, audio); any follow-up/reminder feature (e.g. medication adherence)
   tied to the WHO's **"avoidable harm"**.
4. **Ligação ao enquadramento teórico** — which **HLS-EU-PT health-literacy dimension** the proposal addresses
   (health care / disease prevention / health promotion) and which of the WHO Global Action Plan's **strategic
   objectives SO1–SO7** it links to, and why.
5. **Viabilidade e integração com as outras UCs** — what data the app needs and where it comes from (entered by the
   user, provided by ULSBE, generated by the app); which part can involve **AI** (personalisation, risk detection,
   chatbot); technical challenges foreseen for the mobile development.
6. **Para o parceiro hospitalar** — what information/validation to request from ULSBE to confirm the identified
   problem is real and a priority.

### Facts from the required readings (use these to justify the proposal)
**HLS-EU-PT (literacia em saúde, PT validation study):**
- **61%** of the Portuguese respondents have a *problematic or inadequate* general health-literacy level
  (worse than the 9-country European average of **49.2%**).
- The weakest dimension is **health promotion (60.2% problematic/inadequate)**; only **44.2%** reach sufficient or
  excellent in the *health care* dimension.
- Most vulnerable groups: **the elderly, people with lower schooling, and the unemployed**.

**WHO Global Patient Safety Action Plan 2021–2030:**
- Vision: *"a world in which no one is harmed in health care"*.
- **1 in 10 patients** suffers an adverse event in hospital care in high-income countries;
  in low/middle-income countries, **134 million adverse events per year with 2.6 million deaths**.
- **7 strategic objectives (SO1–SO7)** — **SO4, "engage and empower patients and families"**, links directly to
  the health-literacy theme of this project.

## Prior art from the partner — MyHSMM (received 2026-09-22)
`moodle-originals/projeto-aplicado/MyHSMM.pdf` — 3-page capture (21/09/2026) of the ULSBE news page
(*"Aplicação para dispositivos móveis «MyHSMM» disponível online"*, published 18/02/2021, page updated 08/04/2025;
developed by **SPMS**, co-financed by SAMA 2020; free on App Store / Google Play / app.gov.pt).
**This is the hospital's own existing app — know it before proposing anything.**
- What it does: appointments/exams/surgeries (consult + notifications, cancel/reschedule), outstanding fees
  (taxas moderadoras), history of medical acts, request reports and exam results, satisfaction surveys,
  complaints/suggestions, hospital news, **emergency waiting times**, on-duty pharmacies, clinical staff list,
  useful service info, GPS to the hospital, + "Direitos e Deveres do Utente" and access-to-information pages.
- Coming/added later: automatic check-in at admission kiosks, SMS notifications, document sharing via **Chave
  Móvel Digital**, MB WAY / bank-reference payments, and updates on a relative in the emergency service.
- **How VendanzaCare differs:** MyHSMM is *administrative and transactional* (agenda, payments, documents).
  It does **not** explain diagnoses, flag alarm signs, or support medication adherence. VendanzaCare's space is
  exactly the **literacy/empowerment** layer — and it can be framed as **complementary to MyHSMM**, not a rival,
  which is also the answer to "why not just use the existing app?" in the pitch.
