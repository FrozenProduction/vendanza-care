# VendanzaCare

**Projeto 50+10** — a mobile app for health literacy and patient safety, built together with
**ULS — Unidade Local de Saúde de Barcelos/Esposende (ULSBE)** as the partner hospital.

> "Tecnologia que traduz a saúde em clareza, capacitando o utente para um cuidado mais seguro."

One project, three curricular units — EST / IPCA, Licenciatura em Engenharia de Sistemas Informáticos, 3rd year:

| UC | What it owns here |
|---|---|
| **Projeto Aplicado (PA)** | requirements with ULSBE, architecture, coordination, report + pitch |
| **Programação de Dispositivos Móveis (PDM)** | the Android application in `app/` |
| **Inteligência Artificial (IA)** | the AI service in `ai/` — plain-language health content, personalisation |

## Theme

Health literacy and patient safety: helping patients and carers understand diagnoses and instructions,
recognise alarm signs, and follow medication correctly. The vulnerable groups named by the HLS-EU-PT study
(elderly, low schooling) drive the design: simple language, images, audio, no jargon.

PDM class, and the first deliverable is **PA02 — Proposta de sistema** (`docs/00-RESUMO-PA.md`).
